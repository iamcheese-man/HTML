How Town of Salem was created

In 2014, Josh Brittain wanted to make a social deduction game that was inspired by the games Mafia and Werewolf.

He wanted to keep the core idea of the games, but set it back to another context: The Salem Witch Trials in the 1600s.

Josh and his co-founder Blake Burns then founded the studio: Blank Media Games.

In its first release, it was browser-only. Then the Steam version came out in December 14th (or 15th, depending on timezones) of 2014, a few months after the browser version. Then, on October 14th of 2018, the mobile (Android,iOS) version was released.

Josh relied on a 30,000$ fundraiser program to release the game on Steam.

After 6 years from 2018, the franchise was transferred to Digital Bandidos.

How is the Game played

The game starts on Day 1 with 15 seconds of intermission before the first night begins. On the first night, roles (like Mafioso, Investigator, Jailor) with night abilities (killing, investigating, executing..) do their part. On the second day, the game shows people who died, how they died and what are their roles (skipped if none). After that, there is a 30 second discussion phase for the investigative roles (Town Investigative) to post their findings in chat. (Rule: Almost all Town Investigative roles need to post on day 2, otherwise they're not credible and most likely fake)

The game then enters the Voting phase after those 30 seconds, the town may do Vote For Role (VFR) in this phase to get info about players. Some roles are unique, so if 2 people claim a unique role, they get into a 1 for 1 (Process of elimination). A maximum of 3 trials are allowed per day, and people are allowed to vote Innocent or Guilty on someone after they "upped" (voting someone up) them. Each time someone lands on the innocent or Tie verdict, they get put down the stand, and the time of voting gets reduced. After voting, the players enter night 2, and the cycle repeats.

Win Conditions can be viewed by clicking the bottom button that has the icon of olive leaves, and the game can go on as long as possible.

What are the other systems (and past ones) of Town of Salem

Town of Salem also has other systems related to moderating, like the Trial System, that only jurors, judges and admins can access AND vote on the verdicts of reports. (To be a juror, you need 150 games played)

There used to be a phpBB forums page for ToS, that was used for reporting, and discussion like Discord. it was shut down at around

2019-2020 due to technical difficulties.
